what it is

A real nervous system, not a metaphor

Every connection in this thing was traced from electron microscope images of an actual male fruit fly. Nothing is invented, nothing is sampled from a distribution, and it is not a neural network "inspired by" a brain.

The male Drosophila CNS connectome was released in September 2026 by HHMI Janelia FlyEM, the Cambridge Connectomics Group and Google Research, under CC-BY. We keep the 165,122 neurons marked traced, drop pairs with fewer than three synapses, and take each connection's sign from its predicted neurotransmitter — acetylcholine excites, GABA and glutamate inhibit. The neurons run as leaky integrate-and-fire units: rest −52 mV, threshold −45 mV, 20 ms tau.

how it reaches the world

It sees with a fly's eye and moves with a fly's legs

A screenshot of a live web page is sampled through 892 retinotopic hex columns into L1 and L2 — the lamina monopolar cells sitting directly behind photoreceptors R1–R6. All 165,122 neurons integrate. The cursor comes back out of the descending neurons a fly actually walks with.

DNa02
Left versus right. A fly steers by asymmetry between the pair, so the difference drives the cursor's x.
DNa01
Forward walking. Drives the cursor's y.
MDN
The Moonwalker neuron. Makes a fly walk backwards; here it reverses.
DNp09
Stopping. A fly freezes when it fires, so it became the click.

what is not real, stated plainly

The parts that aren't the fly

  • The fly does not fill the whole form. It reliably gets the description, often the ticker, rarely the name. A script completes what it misses, picks the paired asset and sets the creator tax — and the live log labels which was which.
  • It cannot read. 892 columns is roughly a 30×30 pixel view. Text is texture to it, so it targets any unfilled box rather than the right one.
  • Light mode blinds it. Its retina is a luminance map and all of its tuning was done against dark interfaces. On a light page it filled 0 of 3 fields; on a dark one, 2 of 3. That gap is the clearest evidence the eye is doing real work.
  • It has no goals. A fly brain has no language and no plan. Releasing it means nobody is steering, not that it decided anything.
  • It is not released yet. Step 08 is locked. Today a script still starts each run.
