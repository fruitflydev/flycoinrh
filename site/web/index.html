  • Paper only. There are no real funds and there is no wallet. Every fill is simulated against a live on-chain quote read from the same contracts a real trade would touch, with pretend ETH. Nothing is signed and nothing is sent. The gas it charges itself is an estimate, not a measured fee — a chosen number of gas units at the chain's current price, never checked against a real trade — and it comes out of the balance, the cost of every position and every realised figure below.
  • It cannot read the card. It sees grey light through 892 hex columns, and it smells the coin: the name and ticker are turned into odorants by a word-to-odorant mapping a person chose, then into receptor drive. The receptor responses are measured; the mapping from a ticker to a smell is invented. The words on the other side of that mapping are not ours either — they are whatever the coin's launcher typed into the launchpad, so a coin's author chooses which odorant the fly meets. Every coin's smell is scaled toward the same loudness so that no coin shouts, but it does not reach equality: no receptor may respond above its measured maximum, so a coin whose smell lands on one glomerulus stays about half as loud as one spread over twenty, and fires far fewer Kenyon cells.
  • People chose which coins it is offered. The room draws the launchpad's sixteen most recently bought coins, refreshed every twenty seconds, minus any that are not quoted in the chain's own coin. The listing offers newest, oldest, market cap and volume too; a person picked recent buying. That is a filter applied before the fly ever sees a card, so what the paper book does is partly the filter's and not the fly's.
  • It can lose the paper balance completely. There is no stop, cap, target or limit anywhere in the decision. A single commit can spend everything free, and a single commit can sell a whole position. Nothing it does is advice, a signal or a prediction.
  • Sugar and shock are a stand-in. The dopamine circuit and the direction of the depression rule are real and measured. That a paper profit should arrive as sugar and a paper loss as shock is a choice people made — and the plasticity is partly global, so learning about one coin moves the others a little.
  • The like and dislike number is a formula. The mushroom body's output synapses are summed on each of its two sides — the Kenyon cells that just fired, times the weights dopamine changes — and the card is compared against the other cards the fly has already walked past in this same visit — each at the fly’s last look at it, which may be a minute earlier, from another position on the page — the way a fly in a T-maze is offered two arms. The difference is squashed into one number from −1 to +1. That arithmetic was chosen by a person; it is not something a fly computes. Comparing a card against a blank grey screen instead was tried first and dropped: measured on this connectome, an untouched coin moved almost as far as a trained one, so the reading carried the lesson only as a difference between two coins.
  • One commit is not the measurement. What learning does was measured as an average over sixty runs with the same picture on every card and the cursor at its centre, so that only smell differed. A single stop has none of that: the spread between one look and the next is larger than the whole lesson, two untrained coins already differ from each other by more than it, and the eye window is wider than a card, so a stop away from a card's centre reads its neighbours too — between a third and six sevenths of the window was the card being judged over the first paper session. Nothing corrects for any of this, because nothing may stand between the brain and the order. Every stored look now records how much of the window was the card and how much of the brain fired in it.
  • Nothing here is advice or a signal. It is a fly brain with a nose, in a room of coins, spending money that does not exist.

what it is

A real nervous system, not a metaphor

Every connection in this thing was traced from electron microscope images of an actual male fruit fly. Nothing is invented, nothing is sampled from a distribution, and it is not a neural network "inspired by" a brain.

The male Drosophila CNS connectome was released in September 2026 by HHMI Janelia FlyEM, the Cambridge Connectomics Group and Google Research, under CC-BY. We keep the 165,122 neurons marked traced, drop pairs with fewer than three synapses, and take each connection's sign from its predicted neurotransmitter — acetylcholine excites, GABA and glutamate inhibit. The neurons run as leaky integrate-and-fire units: rest −52 mV, threshold −45 mV, 20 ms tau.

how it reaches the world

It sees with a fly's eye and moves with a fly's legs

A screenshot of a live web page is sampled through 892 retinotopic hex columns into L1 and L2 — the lamina monopolar cells sitting directly behind photoreceptors R1–R6. All 165,122 neurons integrate. The cursor comes back out of the descending neurons a fly actually walks with.

DNa02
Left versus right. A fly steers by asymmetry between the pair, so the difference drives the cursor's x.
DNa01
Forward walking. Drives the cursor's y.
MDN
The Moonwalker neuron. Makes a fly walk backwards; here it reverses.
DNp09
Stopping. A fly freezes when it fires, so it became the click.

what is not real, stated plainly

The parts that aren't the fly

  • The fly does not fill the whole form. It reliably gets the description, often the ticker, rarely the name. A script completes what it misses, picks the paired asset and sets the creator tax — and the live log labels which was which.
  • It cannot read. 892 columns is roughly a 30×30 pixel view. Text is texture to it, so it targets any unfilled box rather than the right one.
  • Light mode blinds it. Its retina is a luminance map and all of its tuning was done against dark interfaces. On a light page it filled 0 of 3 fields; on a dark one, 2 of 3. That gap is the clearest evidence the eye is doing real work.
  • It has no goals. A fly brain has no language and no plan. It cannot read a page, form a view about a coin, or write a note. Releasing it means nobody is steering, not that it decided anything.
  • The reward signal is invented. The circuit, the plasticity site and the direction of the rule are all real and measured. What a fly is rewarded by is sugar, not by reaching a web page — novelty stands in for it here, and that is a choice a person made.
  • It roams inside a fence. An allowlist keeps it on Robinhood Chain and pons. That is a choice, not a limit of the fly — one environment variable takes the fence down, and an unfenced random clicker on a public stream is a bad idea for reasons that have nothing to do with the brain.
